# Code of conduct

ImGen9 is made by and for people who make games. Everyone is welcome here — in issues, pull requests,
Discussions and on [Discord](https://discord.gg/NwuzX3aZkP) — whatever their experience, language or
background.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Python, git or game art.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Respect other people's art: only share images you made or have the right to share.
- Thai and English are both fine. If you can, add a short line in the other language for others.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Sexual or violent content aimed at someone, or posted where it isn't welcome.
- Sharing someone else's private information.
- Posting other people's art or game files as your own, or asking for help to rip them.
- Spam and repeated self-promotion.

## Reporting

Tell a maintainer privately: a direct message on Discord, or the contact on the maintainer's GitHub
profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban anyone who breaks
these rules, in any project space.

This code of conduct is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
