              How to compare

              Walk / movement

              Lineup + walk: the ground under each character scrolls at its game speed. If the feet slide against the lines, the game speed and the animation's stride don't match. The slide column shows by how much.

              Characters rendered with Blender store the real stride speed (root motion). For 2D sprites, enter it by hand.

              Attack

              Lineup + attack replays everyone in sync. ● HIT marks the damage frame. Compare hit ms in the table: windup = hit ms, recovery = duration − hit.

              In Playground, Space attacks the nearest dummy. The dummy flashes at the hit frame if it's within range and in front of you.

              Scale

              Every character is drawn at metres × px/m, so their sizes are comparable. Render every character with the same --ppm.
